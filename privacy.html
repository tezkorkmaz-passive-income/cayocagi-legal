Privacy Policy: Çay Ocağı

App: Çay Ocağı (iOS and Android)

Developer: Prondi Teknoloji Anonim Şirketi

Contact: okant@prondi.com

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Türkçe (KVKK aydınlatma metni dahil)

1. The short version

  • Çay Ocağı contains the card games Batak and King, played against the computer on your device. The game works without an internet connection.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your settings, the game in progress and your statistics stay on your device. We do not run servers that receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described in section 4. The ad is a small banner at the top of the screen (at the table) or at the bottom (home, records and rules); when a game ends, a medium-sized ad box sits inside the results page, below the result, in place of the banner; there are no full-screen ads. No ad is shown during first-time setup, in the tutorial, in Settings, on the legal pages or on the Pro page.
  • If you buy Pro, the app no longer requests or shows ads.
  • The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own.

2. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following on your device so it can work between sessions. This information is not sent to us.

WhatWhy
Appearance and game settings (design, table theme, card size, hand layout, four-colour deck, one-tap play, rule hint, auto-play of a forced card, animations, haptics, sound, speed)To remember your choices
Your last "New game" choicesTo start quickly with the same setup
The game in progress (one for Batak, one for King)So you can continue it
Your statistics and the results of your recent gamesTo show your records screen
Deal codes and scores of your recent hands (up to 30)So you can replay the same deal
Whether Pro is owned or waiting for approvalSo ads stay off after purchase

If you copy a deal code, it is written to your device clipboard only when you tap the button; the app does not read the clipboard.

You can delete all of this by deleting the app or by clearing its data in your device settings. "Reset data" in Settings deletes your records, statistics and games in progress. If you use device backups (for example Google backup or iCloud), your phone may copy this data into your own backup; that is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

3. Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; it is not a subscription. It removes ads and unlocks two designs and two table themes; it does not change how the game plays. Payment is processed by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your payment card details. The app receives confirmation of the purchase from the store and saves on your device only whether Pro is owned.

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads. They are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

What Google may collect. According to Google's own disclosures for its Mobile Ads SDK, when ads are requested the SDK may collect and share:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID on Android, or, on iOS, the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) and app- or developer-scoped identifiers;
  • information about how you interact with the app and with ads (for example app launches, taps and ads viewed);
  • diagnostic and performance information (for example launch time, crash logs, hang rate).

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. On Android, Google's ad software may also use the Android ad services (Topics and measurement APIs) on devices that support them.

What we do not send. The app adds no personal information of its own to ad requests. We do not send your games, statistics or settings to Google.

Consent and your choices.

  • In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. It is not shown in Turkey.
  • If a privacy options form applies to you, you can reopen it at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • iPhone: the app asks for your permission, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, to use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) so that the ads you see can be more relevant to you. A short message from Google explaining the request may appear first. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", or do not answer, the app and Google do not receive your advertising identifier. The game works the same either way. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on the device).
  • Buying Pro stops ad requests from the app.

Links: Google Privacy Policy ; how Google uses information from apps that use its services ; manage ad personalization

5. Children

Çay Ocağı is a card game for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at okant@prondi.com.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any of your data on our own servers. Information on your device stays until you delete the app, clear its data or use "Reset data". Information collected by Google is kept and handled under Google's privacy policy; use the controls in section 4 to manage it.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone ourselves. The only third parties that receive data from the app are Google, through the advertising and consent software described in section 4, and Apple or Google when you make a purchase.

8. Your rights (including EEA, UK and Switzerland; Turkey)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to restrict or object to its use, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority. Where Google processes data for ads in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, it relies on the consent you give in Google's consent form, and you can withdraw it in Settings > Privacy choices. Because we hold no personal information about you on our servers, most requests can be met by deleting the app or its data and by using Google's controls in section 4. For any question or request, contact okant@prondi.com. Residents of Turkey: see the Turkish version of this page for the notice required by Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

9. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Communication between the app's ad software and Google uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at https://tezkorkmaz-passive-income.github.io/cayocagi-legal/privacy.html. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
